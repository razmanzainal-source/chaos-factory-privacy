Chaos Factory / Defense Protocol

Privacy Policy · Effective 14 November 2026

Chaos Factory (also referred to as Defense Protocol) is an offline tower-defense game published by Razman Digital Labs.

Data collection

The game does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. It does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking SDKs.

Local storage

Game progress, best scores, audio settings, blueprint upgrades, map and difficulty preferences, and purchased entitlement status are stored locally on the device in user://savegame.json. This data stays on the device and can be cleared by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the game.

Optional purchases

The game offers one optional one-time cosmetic product, the Founder's Pack. Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play Billing. The game does not receive, see, or store your name, payment card, billing address, or other payment details. Google Play provides the game with a purchase result and an opaque purchase token so the cosmetic can be unlocked and restored; that entitlement is stored only on your device. Google's handling of payment data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. The Founder's Pack is cosmetic only and does not affect gameplay balance.

Permissions

The Android build requests the vibration permission for optional haptic feedback and the internet permission solely for Google Play Billing. The game does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, or storage permissions, and does not send gameplay data over the network.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect information from children, because it does not collect any personal information at all. Purchases require the device's Google Play account and its normal parental controls.
